Full System Reset

Deletes all registered students, pending registration requests, complaints history, and marks all 369 seats as vacant.

Releases all 369 seats to vacant. Active membership records are preserved, but changed to non-reserved status.

Releases all seats to vacant for a specific room only. Active member records in that room are preserved but changed to non-reserved status.

Deletes all student profiles whose membership duration has expired. Their occupied seats will also be vacated.
